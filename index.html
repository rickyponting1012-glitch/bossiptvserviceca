Tips for the Best Streaming Quality

Even the best IPTV service needs a healthy home network. These small steps help a lot.

Use a stable internet connection of about 10 Mbps for one HD stream, and more if many devices are in use. Place your router in an open area. Use a cable where possible. Restart your router now and then. Keep your app updated. If you stream during busy evening hours, close other heavy downloads.

These habits cost nothing, yet they make the viewing experience much smoother.
